1. Introduction

CampusNest (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is a mobile application that helps students find housing near campus and allows agents and landlords to list properties. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using CampusNest, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored only as a secure one-way hash; we never store plain-text passwords)
  • Account role (Student, Agent, or Admin)
  • Optional: phone number, university name, profile photo

Property and listing information (mainly from Agents)

  • Property title, description, type, monthly rent, deposit, location, and amenities
  • Photos uploaded for listings
  • Optional map coordinates

Activity and communications

  • Saved/favorite properties
  • Viewing requests and related details
  • Messages sent between students and agents inside the app
  • Basic activity such as property views

Technical and device information

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • IP address and approximate location derived from network requests
  • Crash reports and diagnostic logs used to fix bugs
We do not sell your personal information.

3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display property listings and search results
  • Enable messaging and viewing requests between students and agents
  • Review and moderate property photos before they appear publicly
  • Improve app performance, security, and reliability
  • Respond to support requests
  • Send important service or security notices
  • Comply with applicable laws and enforce our terms

4. How we share information

We share information only in these limited cases:

With other CampusNest users

  • Students can see agent details shown on a listing (such as name and contact options)
  • Agents can see student details connected to a viewing request
  • Message content is visible only to the people in that conversation

With service providers

  • Cloud hosting, database, and infrastructure providers that process data on our behalf under agreements that require them to protect it

For legal and safety reasons

  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of CampusNest, our users, or others

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own advertising.

5. Photos and content moderation

Photos uploaded by agents may be reviewed by administrators before they are shown publicly. Pending or rejected photos are not displayed in the public feed. We may remove content that violates our rules or applicable law.

6. Data storage and security

  • Data is stored on secure cloud servers
  • Passwords are hashed
  • Access inside the app is limited by role (Student, Agent, Admin)
  • We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information

No system is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential.

7. Data retention

We retain account and listing data while your account remains active. If you request deletion, we will delete or anonymize personal data that we no longer need, except where we must keep certain records for legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

8. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Access personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and related personal data
  • Ask questions about how we process your data

To exercise these rights, email sam4ryan12@protonmail.com from the email address linked to your account. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling the request.

You can also update some profile details inside the app where that feature is available.

9. Children's privacy

CampusNest is intended for users who are able to enter into a binding agreement (generally 18 years of age or older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at sam4ryan12@protonmail.com and we will take steps to delete it.

10. Third-party services and links

CampusNest may use third-party infrastructure (for example hosting and databases) and may open external links such as maps. Those services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the practices of websites or services we do not control.

11. International data processing

If you use CampusNest from a country other than where our servers or service providers operate, your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries. Those countries may have different data-protection laws than your own.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top. Continued use of CampusNest after an update means you accept the revised policy. For material changes, we may provide additional notice in the app or by email when appropriate.
